## What WealthGenie still needs before adding any issuer to live top three

1. Exact active legal product and direct/growth or ETF security identity, issuer
   and source-instrument ID (ISIN / AMFI scheme code or genuine offered contract).
2. Specific official scheme documents, TER/fee, tracking difference/volume,
   subscription and settlement rules, risk evidence, legal tax class and dates.
3. Reviewed commercial rights to store, derive and publicly show the actual
   requested market fields. A regulator's index listing and free website are
   **not** those rights.
4. Approval records in the offline PostgreSQL qualification registry, with
   expiry and profile suitability checks. The new programmatic family prefilter
   discovers only families with three currently source-qualified candidates.
5. No unverified filler products, false return rankings or 750-slot auto-approval.
   An absent full trio is an unavailable category, not an automatic substitute.

SEBI's February 2026 updated mutual-fund categorization is relevant:
https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/feb-2026/categorization-and-rationalization-of-mutual-fund-schemes_99983.html

NSE Indices listings and product counts can change. Recheck before onboarding.
