# SEBI (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 2026 — commencement

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (Mutual Funds) Regulations, 2026 took effect on 1 April 2026. SEBI's consolidated text notes that the original regulations were published in the Gazette of India on 15 January 2026. The cited consolidated edition incorporates amendments through 10 October 2026.

This source verifies the identity and start date of the regulatory framework. It does not establish the current NAV, expected returns, scheme fees, tax treatment, or suitability of an individual mutual fund. SEBI regulation does not mean that a fund's returns are guaranteed.
