# RBI Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2026 — commencement

The Reserve Bank of India FAQ dated 1 July 2026 states that the Reserve Bank - Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, 2026 became effective on 1 July 2026 and replaced the 2021 scheme. The scheme offers a cost-free, non-adversarial redress process for eligible complaints about service deficiencies involving regulated entities covered by the scheme.

RBI identifies separate transition rules for complaints received before 1 July 2026, appeals arising under the 2021 scheme and the execution of previous awards. This short note does not decide whether any individual complaint qualifies, guarantee compensation, or replace RBI's current complaint procedure.
